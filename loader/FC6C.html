FC6C: Initialisation
Entry point after the tape has loaded (Speedlock-protected TZX; the BASIC loader pokes the custom load routine and RANDOMIZEs into it). Also called from GameStart after the source copy at InitialisationSource is relocated here.
Initialisation FC6C LD HL,$FE3B HL=FE3B.
FC6F LD ($FC87),HL Write HL to *FC87.
FC72 LD HL,$FF19 HL=FF19.

FC7C CALL $028E Call KEY_SCAN.
