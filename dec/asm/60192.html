60192: Print Score Digits
Used by the routines at Initialise_Room_Display and Update_HighScoreDisplay.
Print the score in HL as four digits via Print_Digit, suppressing leading zeros until a non-zero digit (or the units) using Flag_ScoreLeadingZeroSuppress.
Print_ScoreDigits 60192 XOR A Clear A to 0.
