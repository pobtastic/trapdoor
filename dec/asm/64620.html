64620: Seed Menu Music And Wait For Key
Used by the routines at GameStart and Print_StatusMessages.
Live copy of the menu-music and key-wait code, relocated from MenuMusicSource by GameStart (length 0915). On game start this entry seeds the two music-stream cursors, then continues into Wait_KeyWithMenuMusic. Menu code also enters at Wait_KeyWithMenuMusic for a single wait tick. Pulse_MenuTiming briefly patches 64640 while pulsing the menu highlight.
Seed_MenuMusicAndWaitForKey 64620 LD HL,65083 Point HL at MenuMusic_StreamOneBase and seed the cursor word at 64647.
64623 LD (64647),HL
64626 LD HL,65305 Point HL at MenuMusic_StreamTwoBase and seed the cursor word at 64651.
64629 LD (64651),HL
