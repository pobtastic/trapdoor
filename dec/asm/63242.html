63242: Colour-Cycle Main Menu Logo
Used by the routines at MainMenu, MainMenu_SelectOptions and Print_ControlKeyLabels.
Cycle attributes across the eight logo cells at Table_MainMenuLogoAttributes. Picks a fresh paper value via PseudoRandom (avoiding a repeat of the scratch byte at Font_MainMenuLogo), then paints a 6×9 attribute block at each cell. Used after Draw_MainMenuLogo and as a menu acknowledge flash from MainMenu_SelectOptions.
Walk all eight logo cells with a new random attribute.
