62141: Test Entity Overlap (Allow Special)
Used by the routine at Update_Entity_Slot.
Find a simple entity at StateData_SimpleEntities whose bounds overlap IX, allowing entities with bit 5 set. On a hit, clear the miss marker, store the entity pointer at Pointer_LastCollidedEntity and return its type in A; on a miss, set carry.
Input
IX Address of complex state data for the subject entity
Test_EntityOverlap_AllowSpecial 62141 LD BC,256 Load BC with 0256 (allow bit 5, then same room).
62144 JR Test_EntityOverlap_Scan Jump to Test_EntityOverlap_Scan to scan with the allow-special mask.
