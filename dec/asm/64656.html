64656: Read Music Stream Byte

Advance a word cursor at HL, fetch the next stream byte, and treat 64 as an end marker (then jump to Reload_MusicStreamCursor to reload the cursor from the following word).

64657 INC HL Advance HL onto the high byte.
64658 LD D,(HL) Fetch the high byte of the cursor word into D.
64659 INC DE Advance the cursor value in DE by one.
