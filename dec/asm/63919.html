63919: Play Queued Sound
Used by the routines at GameStart, Update_Berk_State, Handle_Berk_Action, Print_EncodedMessage and Clear_AttributeFile.
Play the sound queued at PendingSoundIndex using the parameter block selected from base 63751 (sound 1 starts at Table_SoundParameters), then clear the queue. The base 63751 is the operand byte of JR NZ in Queue_Sound; it is never read as a parameter because indexes start at 1.
Fetch the pending sound and return if the queue is empty.
