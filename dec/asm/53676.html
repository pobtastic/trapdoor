53676: Script Routine: (64) Vat shift right when clear above
Probe above with Test_EntityAbove. On a clear miss (carry set) jump to ScriptRoutine_VatIncXIfClearAbove_IncX to shift the vat right, then drop Y at ScriptRoutine_VatDecY. On a hit, if the distance in A is still under 50 continue into that same nudge; otherwise jump straight to ScriptRoutine_VatDecY to drop Y only.
