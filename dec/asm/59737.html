59737: Graphic Layout Routine: (252) Conditional Jump Or Skip
Conditionally jump in the layout, or skip the following address when the test fails.
GraphicLayoutRoutine_ConditionalJumpOrSkip 59737 LD BC,37019 Point BC at the camera-follow entity (StateData_Berk).
59740 INC BC Advance BC past the first two bytes of the camera-follow entity.
59741 INC BC
59742 LD A,(BC) Load A from the byte at BC.
59743 BIT 4,A Is bit 4 of A set? If so, jump to GraphicLayoutRoutine_JumpGraphicAddress.
59745 JP NZ,GraphicLayoutRoutine_JumpGraphicAddress
59748 JP Advance_GraphicLayoutPointerByTwo Jump to Advance_GraphicLayoutPointerByTwo to advance the graphic layout pointer and continue.
