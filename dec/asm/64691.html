64691: Play Menu Music Tick
Used by the routine at Wait_KeyWithMenuMusic.
One tick of the two-channel menu tune while the key-scan waits. Reads both stream cursors, looks each byte up in Table_MenuMusicDelay, then either bit-bangs the speaker on port 254 (toggling bit 4 via 16) or takes the Continue_MenuMusicTick continue path. Called every pass of Wait_KeyWithMenuMusic_Loop.
