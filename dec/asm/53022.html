53022: Update Crusher
Used by the routine at Update_Entity_Slot.
Called from Update_Entity_Slot on the crusher entity path: set bit 4 of EntityInteractionFlag, advance the shared tick at SharedTick, and once that tick reaches 3 also set bit 6. Bit 7 is set on every call.
Mark Berk crushed and advance the shared tick.
