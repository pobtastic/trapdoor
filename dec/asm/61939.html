61939: Update Camera Follow
Used by the routine at GameStart.
Adjust the camera window at Pointer_CameraRoomOrigin to follow the target entity.
If the camera-follow delay is non-zero, tick it down; otherwise scroll the window toward the target.
Update_CameraFollow 61939 LD A,(29450) Load A with the camera-follow delay (CameraFollowDelay).
61942 OR A Is the camera-follow delay (CameraFollowDelay) zero? If so, jump to Update_CameraFollow_Active.
61943 JR Z,Update_CameraFollow_Active
61945 DEC A Decrease A by one.
61946 LD (29450),A Set the camera-follow delay (CameraFollowDelay).
61949 RET Return.
Camera delay expired: point at the follow entity (StateData_Berk) and track it against Pointer_CameraRoomOrigin.
Update_CameraFollow_Active 61950 LD IX,37019 Point IX at the camera-follow entity (StateData_Berk).
61954 LD DE,(29435) Load DE with the camera room-origin pointer (Pointer_CameraRoomOrigin).
61958 LD B,32 Load B with loop count 32.
Probe whether the follow entity sits inside the camera X window.
Update_CameraFollow_ProbeX 61960 LD A,(IX+7) Load A with the current entity's x-coordinate.
61963 ADD A,2 A+=2.
61965 CP E Is A less than E? If so, jump to Update_CameraFollow_ScrollLeft.
61966 JR C,Update_CameraFollow_ScrollLeft
61968 LD C,A Keep A in C for the next calculation.
61969 LD A,D Copy D into A for later use.
61970 CP C Is A less than C? If so, jump to Update_CameraFollow_ScrollRight.
61971 JR C,Update_CameraFollow_ScrollRight
61973 RET Return.
Scroll the camera origin right by 32 until the follow entity is centred.
Update_CameraFollow_ScrollRight 61974 LD A,E Copy the saved low byte from E into A.
61975 CP 160 Is A equal to 160? If so, jump to Update_CameraFollow_WrapLeft.
61977 JR Z,Update_CameraFollow_WrapLeft
61979 ADD A,B A+=B.
61980 LD E,A Copy A into E as a saved coordinate / value.
61981 LD D,A Copy A into D as a saved coordinate / value.
61982 ADD A,B A+=B.
61983 LD D,A Copy A into D as a saved coordinate / value.
61984 LD (29435),DE Store DE in Pointer: Camera Room Origin (Pointer_CameraRoomOrigin).
61988 JR Update_CameraFollow_ProbeX Jump to Update_CameraFollow_ProbeX to continue the loop.
