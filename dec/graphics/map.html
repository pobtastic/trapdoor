Map

The castle rooms are drawn with the same path as Rooms. Clear both display buffers at Clear_BothDisplayBuffers, populate them at PopulateDisplayBuffer_VisibleEntities (room passes 0-5 over the scenery table at StateData_SimpleEntities), then blit at Blit_DisplayBuffer. Camera edges come from Pointer_CameraRoomOrigin.

Placement follows the C64-Wiki map for the Spectrum layout: kitchen on the left, stairs on the right, and the swamp under the weight room. The camera code at Update_CameraFollow steps Pointer_CameraRoomOrigin by 32 between 32 and 160 (five upper rooms). The weight-to-stairs wrap is at Update_CameraFollow_WrapLeft, and the stairs-to-weight wrap is at Update_CameraFollow_WrapRight. Kitchen and stairs are a normal step apart. The swamp camera at 224-255 is entered from the weight room via the white arch entity at StateData_ScenerySecondary (Berk_ReadControls_DownInteract-46981).
