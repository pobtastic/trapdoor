FC6C: Seed Menu Music And Wait For Key
Used by the routines at GameStart and Print_StatusMessages.
Live copy of the menu-music and key-wait code, relocated from MenuMusicSource by GameStart (length 0393). On game start this entry seeds the two music-stream cursors, then continues into Wait_KeyWithMenuMusic. Menu code also enters at Wait_KeyWithMenuMusic for a single wait tick. Pulse_MenuTiming briefly patches FC80 while pulsing the menu highlight.
Seed_MenuMusicAndWaitForKey FC6C LD HL,$FE3B Point HL at MenuMusic_StreamOneBase and seed the cursor word at FC87.
FC6F LD ($FC87),HL
FC72 LD HL,$FF19 Point HL at MenuMusic_StreamTwoBase and seed the cursor word at FC8B.
FC75 LD ($FC8B),HL
Continue on to Wait_KeyWithMenuMusic.
