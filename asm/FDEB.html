FDEB: Play Menu Music Coda
Used by the routine at Continue_MenuMusicTick.
Play coda notes from the table at MenuMusic_CodaNoteTable via the ROM beeper.
Play_MenuMusicCoda FDEB LD E,B Point DE at offset B and HL at MenuMusic_CodaNoteTable.
FDEC LD D,$00
FDEE LD HL,$FE23
FDF1 ADC HL,DE Add the offset into HL.
FDF3 LD A,(HL) Fetch the note length into B.
FDF4 LD B,A
FDF5 LD HL,$0003 Point HL at the starting pitch (0003).
Sound each coda step through Sound_CodaBeep.
Play_MenuMusicCoda_Loop FDF8 PUSH BC Stash the remaining note count on the stack.
FDF9 LD DE,$0001 Load DE with pitch step 0001.
FDFC PUSH HL Stash the current pitch on the stack.
FDFD CALL Sound_CodaBeep Call Sound_CodaBeep to beep at this pitch.
FE00 POP HL Restore the pitch into HL.
FE01 LD DE,$00FF Raise the pitch by 00FF.
FE04 ADC HL,DE
FE06 POP BC Restore the remaining note count.
FE07 DJNZ Play_MenuMusicCoda_Loop Decrease B and loop to Play_MenuMusicCoda_Loop while notes remain.
FE09 RET Return.
