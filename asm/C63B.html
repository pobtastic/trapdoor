C63B: Update Stove
Used by the routine at Update_Entity_Slot.
When the collided entity's type is 14 and the current entity's type is 09. Clears SlimyLoose, BoilEmUp and BoilEmUpLatch; raises StoveUpdated; advances StoveTick (setting StoveReady on the fourth tick); and repoints the cauldron at idle layout GraphicLayout_Cauldron.
Clear SlimyLoose / BoilEmUp / BoilEmUpLatch; raise StoveUpdated.
