E959: Graphic Layout Routine: (FC) Conditional Jump Or Skip
Conditionally jump in the layout, or skip the following address when the test fails.
GraphicLayoutRoutine_ConditionalJumpOrSkip E959 LD BC,$909B Point BC at the camera-follow entity (StateData_Berk).
E95C INC BC Advance BC past the first two bytes of the camera-follow entity.
E95D INC BC
E95E LD A,(BC) Load A from the byte at BC.
E95F BIT 4,A Is bit 04 of A set? If so, jump to GraphicLayoutRoutine_JumpGraphicAddress.
E961 JP NZ,GraphicLayoutRoutine_JumpGraphicAddress
E964 JP Advance_GraphicLayoutPointerByTwo Jump to Advance_GraphicLayoutPointerByTwo to advance the graphic layout pointer and continue.
