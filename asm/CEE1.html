CEE1: Update Plant Pots
Used by the routines at Update_Berk_ReadControls and Update_Ghosts.
Spawn a plant shoot into the first free collectible-eye slot when a pot germinates. IX addresses the pot; free slots are sought from StateData_Complex_07 onwards.
Input
IX Address of the plant-pot complex state data
C Vertical offset applied when placing the shoot above the pot
Stash DE (caller-preserved) while the free eye slot is found.
Update_Plant_Pots CEE1 PUSH DE Stash DE on the stack.
CEE2 LD IY,$8F4B Point IY at the first collectible-eye slot (StateData_Complex_07).
CEE6 LD DE,$000E Each entity record is 000E bytes long, then load that length into DE.
Find the first free collectible-eye slot.
Update_Plant_Pots_FindFreeSlot CEE9 LD A,(IY+$00) Fetch this eye slot's entity type, then is the slot empty (type 00)?.
CEEC OR A
CEED JR Z,Update_Plant_Pots_PlaceShoot If so, plant the shoot here at Update_Plant_Pots_PlaceShoot.
CEEF ADD IY,DE Otherwise advance IY to the next entity record.
CEF1 JR Update_Plant_Pots_FindFreeSlot Keep scanning at Update_Plant_Pots_FindFreeSlot.
Place the shoot in that slot, matching the pot's room and position.
Update_Plant_Pots_PlaceShoot CEF3 LD (IY+$00),$0E Mark the free slot as a plant shoot (type 0E).
CEF7 LD A,(IX+$05) Load the pot's y-coordinate.
CEFA SUB C Raise the shoot by the caller's offset in C.
CEFB LD (IY+$05),A Write that as the shoot's y-coordinate.
CEFE ADD A,$02 Make the shoot two characters tall.
CF00 LD (IY+$06),A Write the shoot's y-extent.
CF03 LD A,(IX+$07) Fetch the pot's x-coordinate.
CF06 LD (IY+$07),A Place the shoot on the same x.
CF09 ADD A,$02 Make the shoot two characters wide.
CF0B LD (IY+$08),A Write the shoot's x-extent.
CF0E LD A,(IX+$04) Fetch the pot's room index.
CF11 LD (IY+$04),A Keep the shoot in the same room as the pot.
CF14 LD (IY+$0A),$1B Set the shoot's graphic layout to GraphicLayout_CollectibleEyeballFrame2.
CF18 LD (IY+$0B),$A1
CF1C POP DE Restore DE from the stack.
CF1D RET Return.
