EDF8: Close Trap Door
Used by the routine at ScriptRoutine_CloseTrapDoor.
Close the trap door: queue a sound, switch the trap-door graphic layout to the closed variant, and update the related scenery entity types.

EDFA PUSH HL Stash HL on the stack.
EDFB PUSH DE Stash DE on the stack.

EDFF JP NC,TrapDoor_RestoreAndReturn Jump to TrapDoor_RestoreAndReturn to continue if no close is required.
