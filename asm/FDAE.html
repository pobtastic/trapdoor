FDAE: Pulse Menu Music Speaker
Used by the routine at Continue_MenuMusicTick.
Pulse the speaker bit on port FE for the delay count built by Build_MusicDelayCount. Walks the step mask in D, writing high or low levels or burning timing for clear bits.
Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker FDAE PUSH AF Stash AF, HL and BC on the stack.
FDAF PUSH HL
FDB0 PUSH BC
FDB1 LD A,($FC86) Load the border and speaker bits from FC86 and clear B.
FDB4 LD B,$00
FDB6 LD HL,$03E8 Point HL at the pulse period base (03E8).
Walk the step mask in D and either pulse or burn timing.
Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Step FDB9 RRC D Rotate D right through carry.
FDBB JP NC,Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Burn If carry is clear, jump to Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Burn for a timing burn.
FDBE INC HL Advance the period pointer.
FDBF BIT 0,(HL) If the selected step bit is clear, jump to Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Low to force the speaker low.
FDC1 JP Z,Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Low
FDC4 SET 4,A Force the speaker bit high in A.
FDC6 XOR %10000011 Toggle spare tone bits with 83.
FDC8 XOR %10000011 Toggle spare tone bits with 83 again.
Write the speaker level and count down the pulse delay.
Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Write FDCA OUT ($FE),A Write the border and speaker bits to port FE.
Inner delay between speaker writes.
Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Delay FDCC NOP Timing pad.
FDCD DEC B Decrease B and loop to Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Step while the fine count remains.
FDCE JP NZ,Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Step
FDD1 DEC C Decrease C and loop to Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Step while the coarse count remains.
FDD2 JP NZ,Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Step
FDD5 POP BC Restore BC, HL and AF from the stack.
FDD6 POP HL
FDD7 POP AF
FDD8 RET Return.
Force the speaker bit low, then write it.
Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Low FDD9 RES 4,A Clear the speaker bit in A.
FDDB JR Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Write Jump to Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Write to write it.
Timing burn for a clear step-mask bit.
Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Burn FDDD SCF Set the carry flag.
FDDE JP NC,$0000 Timing pad (JP NC).
FDE1 JP NC,$0000 Timing pad (JP NC).
FDE4 JP NC,$0000 No operations.
FDE7 NOP
FDE8 NOP
FDE9 JR Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Delay Jump to Pulse_MenuMusicSpeaker_Delay to resume the delay count.
