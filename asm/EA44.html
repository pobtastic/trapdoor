EA44: Clear Display Buffer
Used by the routine at GameStart.
Clear the current display buffer.
Clear_DisplayBuffer EA44 LD HL,($72F5) Load HL with the current display-buffer pointer (Pointer_CurrentDisplayBuffer).
EA47 LD BC,$077F Load BC with 077F.
This entry point is used by the routine at Clear_BothDisplayBuffers.
Shared zero-fill used to clear one or both display buffers.
Fill_MemoryWithZero EA4A XOR A Clear A to 00.
EA4B LD (HL),A Set the byte at HL.
EA4C LD D,H Copy HL to DE.
EA4D LD E,L Copy HL to DE.
EA4E INC DE Advance the clear-attributes destination by one.
EA4F LDIR Copy bytes from *HL to *DE until BC is zero.
EA51 RET Return.
