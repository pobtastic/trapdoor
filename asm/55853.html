DA2D: Draw Dialogue Character
Used by the routine at Continue_Bad_Tempered_Thing_Dialogue.
Draw one ROM/charset glyph for Bad Tempered Thing dialogue. HL is the destination attribute-file address; E holds the character code. Thickens the bitmap (byte OR byte>>01) and points Berk's graphic layout at GraphicLayoutScript_BerkActions while the line is shown.
