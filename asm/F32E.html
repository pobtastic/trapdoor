F32E: Test Entity On Right
Used by the routines at Berk_Interact_With_Entity and ScriptRoutine_TaskThreeClearUpstairs.
Find a solid simple entity immediately to the right of IX (for a rightward step). On a hit, store it at Pointer_LastCollidedEntity and clear carry; on a miss, set carry.
Input
IX Address of complex state data for the subject entity
Test_EntityOnRight F32E LD C,$01 Load C with 01 (room−01 / right bias).
This entry point is used by the routine at Test_EntityOnLeft.
