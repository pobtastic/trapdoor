B7F3: Berk Interact With Entity
Used by the routine at Update_Berk_ReadControls.
Test horizontal alignment with the last-collided entity (Pointer_LastCollidedEntity); on match clear bit 02 of IX+03.
Input
IX Address of complex state data for an entity
Handles Berk colliding with doors, the trap door and interactive scenery.
