BE6D: Data: Bird Scripts
Bird scripts that drive the bird laying an egg. BF38 starts here. Other readers enter at BirdScript_RiseLoop, BirdScript_IdleLoop, BirdScript_TurnLoop, BirdScript_Wander, BirdScript_BoundLoop and BirdScript_AltWander. BF68 pokes the wander loop count at BE9B. Ends immediately before Update_Task_Two_Entities.
Start script published by Update_TaskTwo_StartEntity and BF38.
