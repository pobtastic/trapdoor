F391: Test Entity Below
Used by the routines at Berk_Pick_Up_Entity and ScriptRoutine_TaskThreeClearUpstairs.
Find a solid simple entity immediately below IX (for a downward step). On a hit, store it at Pointer_LastCollidedEntity and clear carry; on a miss, set carry.
Input
IX Address of complex state data for the subject entity
Test_EntityBelow F391 LD C,$01 Load C with 01 (room−01 / down bias).
