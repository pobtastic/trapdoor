FC90: Read Music Stream Byte
Used by the routine at Play_MenuMusicTick.
Advance a word cursor at HL, fetch the next stream byte, and treat 40 as an end marker (then jump to Reload_MusicStreamCursor to reload the cursor from the following word).
Read_MusicStreamByte FC90 LD E,(HL) Fetch the low byte of the cursor word into E.
FC91 INC HL Advance HL onto the high byte.
FC92 LD D,(HL) Fetch the high byte of the cursor word into D.
FC93 INC DE Advance the cursor value in DE by one.
This entry point is used by the routine at Reload_MusicStreamCursor.
Fetch the byte at the stream cursor.
Read_MusicStreamByte_Fetch FC94 LD A,(DE) Fetch the byte at the cursor.
FC95 CP $40 Is it the end marker 40?
FC97 JR Z,Reload_MusicStreamCursor If so, jump to Reload_MusicStreamCursor to reload the cursor from the next word.
FC99 LD (HL),D Write the updated high byte back.
FC9A DEC HL Step HL back to the low byte.
FC9B LD (HL),E Write the updated low byte back.
FC9C RET Return.
