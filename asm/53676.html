D1AC: Script Routine: (40) Vat shift right when clear above
Probe above with Test_EntityAbove. On a clear miss (carry set) jump to ScriptRoutine_VatIncXIfClearAbove_IncX to shift the vat right, then drop Y at ScriptRoutine_VatDecY. On a hit, if the distance in A is still under 32 fall through into that same nudge; otherwise jump straight to ScriptRoutine_VatDecY to drop Y only.
Input
IX Address of complex state data for an entity
Probe above; carry set = miss (path clear).
ScriptRoutine_VatIncXIfClearAbove D1AC CALL Test_EntityAbove Call Test_EntityAbove to test whether a solid entity sits above this vat.
D1AF JR C,ScriptRoutine_VatIncXIfClearAbove_IncX Was the space above clear (carry set)? If so, jump to ScriptRoutine_VatIncXIfClearAbove_IncX to shift right.
D1B1 CP $32 On a hit: is the distance in A still under 32? If not, jump to ScriptRoutine_VatDecY to drop Y only (skip the nudge).
D1B3 JR NC,ScriptRoutine_VatDecY
Shift right (reached by jump on miss, or fall-through when A<32), then drop Y.
ScriptRoutine_VatIncXIfClearAbove_IncX D1B5 INC (IX+$07) Move the vat one character right.
D1B8 INC (IX+$08)
D1BB JR ScriptRoutine_VatDecY Jump to ScriptRoutine_VatDecY to decrease y / y-extent.
