FE0A: Sound Coda Beep
Used by the routine at Play_MenuMusicCoda.
Finalise one coda step: scale the pitch, index the ROM beeper helper, then return with interrupts disabled.
Sound_CodaBeep FE0A LD A,L Copy the pitch low byte into A.
FE0B SRL L Divide L by 04 with two logical shifts right.
FE0D SRL L
FE0F CPL Complement A.
FE10 AND %00000011 Keep only the bottom two bits of A.
FE12 LD C,A Copy that index into C and clear B.
FE13 LD B,$00
FE15 LD IX,$03D1 Point IX at BEEPER and index it by BC.
FE19 ADD IX,BC
FE1B LD A,($FC86) Load the border and speaker bits from FC86.
FE1E CALL $03D4 Call BEEPER.
FE21 DI Disable interrupts.
FE22 RET Return.
