72C2: Shared Tick
One-byte counter shared by Update_Crusher (increments on each crusher update) and script routine ScriptRoutine_TaskFourTickOfferItem (decrements toward zero). Not a pure frame counter: Update_Crusher advances it once per crusher call, and when the value reaches 03 that routine also sets bits 06 and 07 of EntityInteractionFlag.
