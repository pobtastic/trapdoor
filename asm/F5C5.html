F5C5: Print Control Key Labels (Short)
Used by the routine at MainMenu_SelectOptions.
Entry that reprints the first 05 control-key labels using attribute A (used when JOYSTICK is chosen to clear the KEYS column). Jumps into Print_ControlKeyLabels at Print_ControlKeyLabels_WalkTable with a five-label count.
Print_ControlKeyLabels_Short F5C5 LD B,$05 Load B with 05 label count.
F5C7 XOR A Clear A (blank / black attribute).
F5C8 JR Print_ControlKeyLabels_WalkTable Jump to Print_ControlKeyLabels_WalkTable to print with a blank attribute and five labels.
