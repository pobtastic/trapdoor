F1A1: Script Routine: (12) Position put-down entity to the left
Place the put-down entity to the carrier's left.
Input
IX Address of complex state data for an entity
ScriptRoutine_PlacePutDownLeft F1A1 BIT 4,(IX+$03) Is bit 04 of flags byte 3 (IX+03) set? If not, jump to AdvanceScriptInstruction.
F1A5 JP Z,AdvanceScriptInstruction
F1A8 LD A,($72ED) Load A with the saved held-entity width from CameraYCoordinate.
F1AB LD D,A Copy A into D as a saved coordinate / value.
F1AC INC A Step the put-down placement index on by one.
F1AD SRL A Shift A right logical.
F1AF LD E,A Copy A into E as a saved coordinate / value.
F1B0 XOR A Clear A to 00.
F1B1 SUB E Subtract E from A.
F1B2 ADD A,(IX+$07) Add the entity byte at offset 07 to A.
F1B5 JR ScriptRoutine_PlacePutDownLeft_WritePutDown Jump to ScriptRoutine_PlacePutDownLeft_WritePutDown to script Routine 12 Write Put Down.
