AD98: Clear Messaging Attributes
Used by the routines at Update_Berk_State and Handle_Berk_Action.
Blank the attribute cells behind the status / menu message area so the next printed line starts on a clean strip.
Clear_Messaging_Attributes AD98 LD BC,$0000 Clear BC (zero length/offset).
Clear_Messaging_Attributes_Countdown AD9B DEC BC Step the length/counter in BC back by one.
AD9C LD A,B Copy the loop counter from B into A.
AD9D OR C OR C into A to test whether BC has reached zero.
AD9E JR NZ,Clear_Messaging_Attributes_Countdown If the result was non-zero, jump to Clear_Messaging_Attributes_Countdown.
ADA0 RET Return.
