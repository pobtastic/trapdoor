E286: Print Encoded Message
Used by the routines at Print_TooLateTaskFeedback, Print_StatusMessages, Print_MessageString, Print_DeathMessage, Print_TaskProgressIntro and Print_WagesMessage.
Print one […] encoded message at HL. First runs a short border/sound flash, then filters control bytes into the display buffer at PrimaryDisplayBuffer and jumps to Paint_MessageBuffer to paint it. Control bytes before ` gate fragments by MessageTrigger / MessageColour; + starts a new row; $ marks an alternate fragment.
Input
HL Address of an encoded message (starts at [)
Flash / click 0A times before decoding.
Print_EncodedMessage E286 PUSH HL Stash HL on the stack.
E287 LD B,$0A Load B with 0A flash iterations.
Print_EncodedMessage_FlashLoop E289 PUSH BC Stash BC on the stack.
E28A LD A,$10 Pick a pseudo-random flash/sound parameter via PseudoRandom and store it at F958.
E28C CALL PseudoRandom
E28F ADD A,$14
E291 LD ($F958),A
E294 LD A,$0A Play sound effect 0A via Queue_Sound / Play_QueuedSound.
E296 CALL Queue_Sound
E299 CALL Play_QueuedSound
E29C POP BC Restore BC from the stack.
E29D DJNZ Print_EncodedMessage_FlashLoop Loop until the flash sequence is done.
E29F POP HL Restore HL from the stack.
