90B7: State Data: Scenery Entities
Room scenery and fixture state records (000E bytes each, terminated by FF at 9207). These are not included in the Backup_Entity_And_Game_State backup. Field names match StateData_SimpleEntities: flags byte 1 at F2EA, flags byte 2 at B1BE, flags byte 3 at Update_Berk_ReadControls, room and position at PopulateDisplayBuffer_VisibleEntitiesContinue, Process_GraphicLayoutData_Entity, F2FA, F301 and F30F, direction and state at B344 and Update_Berk_Movement_And_Script_PartA, and layout at IX+0A. Close_TrapDoor and Open_TrapDoor write the lever layout word at Pointer_TrapDoorLayout when the trap door closes and opens, and ScriptRoutine_AnimateTrapDoorBerk and Reset_Trap_Door_Animation_State write the lever layout word at Pointer_AnimatingSceneryLayout.
