EDDB: Open Trap Door
Used by the routines at Berk_Interact_With_Entity and ScriptRoutine_OpenTrapDoor.
Open the trap door: queue a sound, switch the trap-door graphic layout to the open variant, and update the related scenery entity types.
Open_TrapDoor EDDB PUSH IX Stash IX on the stack.
EDDD PUSH HL Stash HL on the stack.
EDDE PUSH DE Stash DE on the stack.
EDDF LD HL,$72B3 Point HL at the game event flags (GameEventFlags).
EDE2 BIT 4,(HL) Is "task-two / upstairs event latch" (bit 04 of GameEventFlags) set? If so, jump to TrapDoor_RestoreAndReturn.
EDE4 JR NZ,TrapDoor_RestoreAndReturn
EDE6 RES 1,(HL) Clear "trap door is open / trap-door event armed" (bit 01 of GameEventFlags).
EDE8 LD A,$06 Load A with sound index 06.
EDEA CALL Queue_Sound Call Queue_Sound to queue sound.
EDED LD HL,$A809 Point HL at the open trap-door graphic layout (GraphicLayout_TrapDoorOpen).
EDF0 LD ($9177),HL Store HL in the trap-door layout pointer (Pointer_TrapDoorLayout).
EDF3 LD HL,$EDC9 Point HL at the trap-door open/close type list (Data_TrapDoorOpenTypes).
EDF6 JR UpdateTrapDoorEntityTypes Jump to UpdateTrapDoorEntityTypes to handle the trap door.
